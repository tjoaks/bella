How We Got Here

The Land

It started with a drive up a gravel road in the Smoky Mountains that didn't seem to lead anywhere. Acres of rolling pasture, hardwood forest, and a ridgeline that opened up to a view you could feel in your chest. The kind of place where you stand still for a moment and forget what you were rushing toward.

We didn't know what Bella would become. We just knew we couldn't walk away from it.

The Build

What started as weekend campfires with friends became blueprints on the kitchen table. We cleared trails by hand, raised the first barn with help from neighbors, and spent two full seasons getting the grounds ready before a single guest arrived.

We sourced reclaimed wood from a century-old tobacco barn in the next county. The stone for the ceremony circle came from the creek that runs through the property. Every material has a story, and every structure was placed with the landscape — not against it.

The First Season

Our first wedding was for close friends who believed in the vision before the paint was dry. Forty guests, string lights strung between the oaks, and a dance floor made of hand-laid flagstone. It wasn't perfect — the generator cut out during the first dance and someone's dog got into the cake — but every single person said it was the most beautiful wedding they'd ever attended.

That was the night we knew Bella was exactly what it was supposed to be.

Where We Are Now

Today, Bella hosts over fifty weddings a year, runs a glamping experience that books out months in advance, and welcomes corporate groups, reunions, and festivals to the mountain. We've grown, but we haven't changed. The same people who cleared those first trails still greet you at the gate.

This is still a place built by hand, with heart, one season at a time. And we want you to be part of the story.

Getting Here

From Asheville, take I-40 West toward Waynesville, then continue on US-74 West through the Tuckasegee River valley. Just past Whittier, turn onto Feather Road. You'll know you're close when the road narrows and the mountains close in around you. We'll send a pin and detailed directions when you book.

The drive is part of the experience — winding through the Smokies with rivers running alongside the road, mist settling in the valleys, and ridgelines rising in every direction. By the time you arrive, the rest of the world feels very far away.
