Your Celebration at Bella

Nestled deep in the Great Smoky Mountains of western North Carolina, Bella offers an intimate yet expansive setting for your wedding day. With sweeping views, rustic elegance, and the warmth of nature all around, every moment here feels like it was meant to be.

Capacity Up to 250 guests
Property 80+ acres
Season April – November
Amenities Bridal suite, outdoor ceremony sites, reception barn

From the ceremony to the last dance, our grounds become entirely yours. We work closely with each couple to craft a day that reflects who you are — no two weddings at Bella are ever the same.
